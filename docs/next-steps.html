How A places a task

Urgent means due within 2 days or overdue. Important means P1 or P2; Jira's Highest and High become P1, Medium P2, the rest P3, as the list does today. A task with no due date sits at the far right.

What a drag does

Into Do now: priority P1, due today. Into Later: P3, due date kept. For a Jira ticket Ally writes the priority and due date straight to Jira's REST API, as Move to does now; read-only sign-ins get a notice instead.
